## Overview

Finance AutoMate is an AI-driven personal finance management platform. It automates budgeting using SMS and bank statements, recommends the best investments based on user goals, and handles both short and long-term planning. The app executes investments, sends reminders, and shares relevant financial news – all personalized by AI.

---

## Features

- **Automated Budgeting:** Parses SMS and monthly bank statements to track and analyze expenses.
- **Personalized Investment Recommendations:** Suggests the best investments by understanding user risk profile and financial goals (short-term/long-term).
- **Goal-Based Planning:** Supports life events and specific objectives (retirement, festivals, children's education, major purchases).
- **Automated Transactions & Reminders:** Executes investments, schedules SIPs, and sends timely notifications (e.g., SIP reminders 23–25th of every month).
- **Finance Automation Bot:** AI-powered bot to automate user investments and solve queries.
- **Personalized News Feed:** Delivers curated financial news and scheme updates based on the user's portfolio and interests.
- **Performance Dashboard:** Real-time analytics with expenditure graphs, trend charts, personalized scoring, and suggestions for improvement.
- **Security & Compliance:** Multi-layer authentication, hashing, API security, and adherence to financial regulations.
- **Tax & Compliance Guide:** Helps with tax returns, compliance reminders, and regulatory updates.

## How It Works

1. **Connect Bank & SMS:** User links bank accounts or uploads statements; app reads SMS for real-time expenses.
2. **Analyze & Categorize:** Expenses categorized, visualized, and scored.
3. **Set Goals:** User defines short/long-term targets; AI computes investment roadmap.
4. **Auto-Invest & Remind:** Platform automates monthly/periodic investments, sends smart reminders.
5. **Personalized News & Support:** App pushes relevant investment/news updates and answers queries via bot.
